# 90-second demo script
Practice with the sample week. Speak in your own words. Record the real working app; do not imply you have users or results you have not measured.

0:00–0:12 — Show the plan.
“Coming back after missing classes can mean four subjects and no idea where to begin. Rebound helps you see what fits, what comes next, and when to ask for help.”

0:12–0:30 — Point at the sample label and daily time control.
“This is fictional sample data. I have 45 minutes today and an hour on other days. Rebound splits work into short sessions, with the earliest deadlines first.”

0:30–0:45 — Set today's time to 15 minutes, then restore it to 45.
“If I have less time, it recalculates. It does not hide work that cannot fit: the deadline warning tells me the shortfall.”

0:45–1:00 — Open 'Ask for more time'.
“I can review and edit a request to my teacher. It never sends anything automatically. Asking for a realistic deadline is part of the plan.” Close dialog.

1:00–1:13 — Complete a session, then Undo.
“Finishing a session reduces my remaining work and the time I have left today, so the app does not just refill my day.”

1:13–1:25 — Open Assignments, edit one, save; show backup buttons.
“I can change estimates, save a backup, and print the week. My assignments stay in this browser. There are no paid APIs.”

1:25–1:35 — Finish on the plan.
“Codex assisted with the design, code, and tests. I can explain the scheduling logic and its limitations. Our next step is real student testing.”
